The process

One loop, run for every claim you send.

Nothing here waits on new software. It runs by hand, on real claims, starting with the first batch you send.

  1. 01

    You send the claim

    Denial letter, EOB, or underpayment — however you have it. No special format required.

  2. 02

    We review it

    We assess the denial reason, payer pattern, and recoverable exposure, and tell you honestly whether it's worth pursuing. Some won't be — we'll say so.

  3. 03

    We file and track the appeal

    We prepare and submit the appeal, then follow it through the payer's process.

  4. 04

    You get a plain-English summary

    Status, what we did, and what to expect next — no jargon, no guessing.

  5. 05

    Payment lands, then we invoice

    We bill our fee only after the payer pays you. If nothing is recovered, you owe nothing.
