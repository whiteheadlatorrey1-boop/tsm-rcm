## Notes on slide count and pacing
- 10 slides is a lean, tight deck — good for a first meeting or teaser. If you're building a longer data-room version, slides 4–5 (per-vertical product shots) are the easiest to expand — just repeat the pattern for Insurance, Legal, Real Estate, FinOps, BPO using their respective `media/<vertical>/` folders.
- Investors generally skim fast — one strong screenshot per product slide beats three cluttered ones. Resist the urge to show War Room + Strategist + Executive on the same slide; let each carry its own beat.

## Before finalizing
- Every screenshot needs the same review pass as the website structure doc: confirm no placeholder/test data, no debug overlays, consistent browser chrome (or none, if you crop tightly to the app UI).
- Consider a consistent crop/frame treatment (e.g., browser-window mockup, or clean UI-only crop) applied uniformly across all screenshots used in this deck — mixing raw full-page captures with framed ones looks unpolished.
