Presenter talk track — 5–7 minutes

Opening: “The college does not have a data shortage. It has a decision-context problem. Each domain can see its own risk; leadership needs to see how those signals converge.”

Signals: Start with Enrollment at $1.056M, then Financial Aid, Bursar, Research F&A, Accreditation, and Endowment. Emphasize that TSM does not replace the operating workflows.

Sentinel: “This is the control-plane moment. The same domain signals become anomalies with severity, exposure or readiness, and a recommended action.”

Strategist: “Now we stop looking at six queues and start looking at one institutional picture.”

Executive Portal: “The immediate value is not another dashboard. It is the compression of operational signals into a governed executive decision context.”
