# TSM Consultz — Real Estate (RE) Vertical
## Live Demo Script: UNDERSTAND → DECIDE → EXECUTE → TRUST

**Audience:** Prospective partners / buyers (e.g. credit union partnership pitch)
**Runtime:** ~10–12 minutes
**Path:** `re-war-room.html` → `re-strategist.html` → `re-exec-portal.html`
**Chain bar:** every screen keeps a persistent WAR ROOM → STRATEGIST → EXEC PORTAL tracker at the bottom, so the audience never loses the thread.

---

## 0. Open — Set Up the Story (before any click)

**Talk points:**
- "Every real estate shop — retail, investor, mortgage — runs the same three conversations on a loop: what's happening on the ground, what does it mean for the business, and what does leadership need to decide. Today those three conversations live in three different tools, three different spreadsheets, and three different people's heads."
- "What I'm going to show you is one connected system that carries a single deal from the desk that's working it, up through a strategic read, to a board-ready number — automatically, with the same data at every layer."
- "This is the Real Estate vertical inside TSM Consultz — one of eight verticals on the platform. Real estate and mortgage in this build are combined into one operating chain, so you're seeing acquisition, transaction management, mortgage ops, compliance, market intel, and closing all in one war room."

**Action:** Load `re-war-room.html`. Let it sit for two seconds before touching anything.

---

## 1. RE War Room — "This Is Where the Work Happens"

**Talk points:**
- "This is the war room — the operator-level desk. It's organized into seven node groups on the left, each one a functional area of a real estate/mortgage operation."
- Point to the sidebar, reading the node list once: **REA · Acquisition, REAF · Finance, REAT · Transaction, REAM · Mortgage Ops, REAC · Compliance, REAI · Market Intel, REAX · Closing.**
- "Each node has live modules behind it — fourteen in total. These aren't static reports; every one of these is a scoped AI analysis running against real case data the moment you click it."

**Click 1 — `REA · Acquisition` → "Buyer & Seller Intake"**
- **Say while clicking:** "Let's start where every deal starts — intake."
- **After it runs:** "That's lead qualification, seller motivation, and property matching, generated against whatever's loaded in this session — with the specific dollar thresholds an agent would actually use, not generic advice."

**Click 2 — `REAF · Finance` → "Pre-Qualification Engine"**
- **Say:** "Now the finance side of the same deal — pre-qual, without switching tools or re-entering data."

**Click 3 — `REAM · Mortgage Ops` → "Loan Product Compare"**
- **Say:** "And mortgage ops sits right next to it — same war room, same session. This is the piece most platforms treat as a completely separate system. Here it's one node group over."

**Talk point (module grid overview, don't click every card):**
- "There are two modules under each of the seven nodes — appraisal gap rescue, RESPA/TRID audit, FinCEN/AML audit, Phoenix market analysis, BPO valuation, closing readiness — I won't run all fourteen today, but every one of them is live, not a mockup."

---

## 2. Deal Rescue Pack — "This Is Where the Value Shows Up"

**Action:** Click the **DEAL RESCUE PACK** tab (⚡) at the top of the war room.

**Talk points:**
- "This is the feature that gets used the most, because this is where deals actually die — or get saved. Six failure scenarios, and you can select more than one at once because real deals rarely fail for just one reason."

**Click — select scenario buttons:** `FINANCING FAILURE`, then `APPRAISAL GAP`
- **Say:** "Say we've got a deal with a financing issue *and* an appraisal gap — stack them."

**Action:** Fill in the two dollar fields — Earnest Money and Appraisal Gap/Issue Amount.
- **Say:** "Drop in the real numbers at risk — earnest money, the gap amount — so the output is scoped to actual exposure, not a hypothetical."

**Click — `⚡ RUN TRANSACTION RESCUE PACK`**
- **Say:** "One click, and the system builds the rescue plan for both problems together — negotiation tactics, financing workarounds, specific next steps with the dollar amounts baked in."

---

## 3. Build Remediation Mission — "From Analysis to Action"

**Action:** Scroll to the **⚡ BUILD REMEDIATION MISSION** button.

**Talk points:**
- "Analysis is only half the job. This button converts what we just found into a tracked mission — it's not just advice in a text box, it's a work item with an owner and a status, and it's what feeds the cross-vertical risk view (Sentinel Center) that leadership sees across all eight verticals."

**Click — `⚡ BUILD REMEDIATION MISSION`**

---

## 4. Escalate to Strategist — "Handing Off, Not Starting Over"

**Action:** Click **`↗ ESCALATE → STRATEGIST`** (or the equivalent escalate control on the war room).

**Talk points:**
- "This is the handoff. Everything we just did — the intake, the rescue pack, the mission — travels with the deal. The strategist isn't starting from a blank page; they're picking up exactly where the war room left off."

---

## 5. RE Strategist — "This Is the Translation Layer"

**Talk points (on load):**
- "The strategist tier takes operator-level detail and turns it into portfolio-level judgment. Notice the chain bar at the bottom still shows WAR ROOM behind us and EXEC PORTAL ahead — same deal, same thread, different altitude."
- "On the left are eight strategist modules — Deal Rescue Strategy, Portfolio Risk Matrix, Compliance Priority Stack, Pipeline Optimization, Appraisal Gap Playbook, Title Resolution Roadmap, REO Disposition Strategy, and Market Position Analysis."

**Click — `Portfolio Risk Matrix`**
- **Say:** "Let's pull the risk matrix — this scores every active deal across financing, title, inspection, closing-timeline, and compliance risk, and rolls it up into one dollar-exposure number for the whole pipeline."

**Talk points (quick actions sidebar):**
- "There's also a set of one-click strategic questions built in — 'what's the single highest-dollar risk in the next 24 hours,' 'which deals are most likely to fall through in the next 30 days' — these are the questions a VP actually asks in a Monday pipeline review."

**Click — `⚡ FULL STRATEGIC BRIEF`**
- **Say:** "And when it's time to write the whole thing up, one button builds the complete strategic brief — every module's output, synthesized into one document instead of eight separate outputs someone has to stitch together by hand."

---

## 6. Escalate to Exec Portal — "One More Handoff, Same Deal"

**Action:** Click **`↗ ESCALATE → EXEC PORTAL`**.

**Talk points:**
- "Same pattern as before — nothing gets re-entered, nothing gets summarized by a person in between. The strategist's brief becomes the input to the executive view."

---

## 7. RE Exec Portal — "This Is What Leadership Actually Sees"

**Talk points (KPI row, read left to right):**
- "Six live KPI tiles across the top — **Pipeline Volume, Pull-Through Rate against a 75% target, Estimated Revenue, Dollar Risk Exposure, REO Portfolio, and Closings This Week.** Every one of these is computed from real numbers surfaced upstream in the war room and strategist — nothing here is a placeholder or a fabricated figure. That's a hard rule on this platform: no invented dollar values, anywhere in the chain."
- "Below that: **Action Rev Loss** and **Recovery Time** — what it costs if leadership does nothing, versus how fast it recovers if they act on the recommendation."

**Action:** Click through the left-hand panel tabs, narrating each:

| Click | Talk point |
|---|---|
| **Executive Brief** | "The default view — the strategist's full brief, board-ready as written." |
| **Action Items** | "Everything that needs a decision, pulled out of the brief into a checklist." |
| **Pipeline View** | "The transaction pipeline itself, at a glance." |
| **Revenue Forecast** | "Where the numbers are headed if current trends hold." |
| **Deal Snapshot** | "Drill into a single deal without leaving the executive view." |
| **Compliance Flags** | "Every open TRID, HMDA, and FinCEN item, ranked by exposure." |
| **Deal Rescue Pack** | "The same rescue output from the war room, now visible at the executive level — full traceability from operator to board." |
| **REO 60-Day Plan** | "Disposition strategy for the REO portfolio, one click away." |
| **Mortgage Position** | "Product mix, pull-through by product, and a 90-day origination forecast — this is the piece most platforms don't connect to real estate at all." |

**Click — `Mortgage Position`**
- **Say (closing beat):** "This tab is the whole pitch in miniature — mortgage and real estate, historically two separate systems, sitting behind one login, fed by the same deal data, three clicks from the desk where the work actually happened."
