02 · Relay Flow

One relay chain, three pages, no re-entry of data

War Room → Strategist → Executive Portal
Standard 2-key relay pattern
Live
STEP 01
Fire Extraction Engine
War Room extracts sector, doc type, exposure, and engine findings from the supplier notice, then writes the payload to TSM_BPO_WAR_RELAY.
STEP 02
Generate Strategy Brief
Strategist reads that relay, streams a Groq brief with a 30s abort ceiling. On failure it degrades to a real data-grounded fallback brief, not a spinner.
STEP 03
Escalate & Confirm
Strategist writes TSM_BPO_STRAT_RELAY; Executive Portal reads it, confirms, and writes TSM_EXEC_CONFIRMED_bpo to close the loop.

04 · Exception Management

What's live today

TSMExceptions Engine · Sector-Scoped

The BPO Executive Portal feeds the shared TSMExceptions P1/P2/P3 queue, same as Construction and Healthcare. When the executive confirms a strategy, bpoFeedExceptions() calls TSMExceptions.add() for every escalation trigger in the brief — first trigger tagged P1, the rest P2 — and the exception widget remounts immediately so the queue reflects it without a page reload.

What is real: the full three-hop relay chain (War Room → Strategist → Executive Portal), the streamed AI strategy brief with a genuine data-grounded fallback on model failure, the confirm-and-close write at the end, and the exception queue feed that fires on that same confirmation.
