Foundation

Three layers, shared across every part of your business

Instead of rebuilding the same logic for every department, TSM runs on a shared core that every war room draws from.

LAYER 01

Event bus

Every action — a quote created, an order allocated, an invoice issued — becomes a timestamped event. Nothing is overwritten and nothing is lost, so any question about "what happened" has a real answer.

LAYER 02

Governance layer

Access, approvals, and segregation of duties are enforced the same way everywhere. A rule written once for procurement applies the same way in finance, without a separate build.

LAYER 03

AI decision intelligence

The same event stream that gives you an audit trail also feeds anomaly detection, root-cause analysis, and recommendations — so the system flags a duplicate invoice or a stalled approval before it becomes a problem.
