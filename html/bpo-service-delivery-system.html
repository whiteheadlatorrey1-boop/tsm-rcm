Healthcare — Denial & Underpayment Recovery
Client: hospital / practice billing office
▶
01
Intake
Client sends denial letters, EOBs, underpayment notices — any format they already have.
02
Assess
Strategist scores denial reason and payer pattern; BNCA exposure engine flags what's actually worth fighting.
03
Process
Appeal drafted and filed, tracked through the payer's resubmission cycle.
04
QA
Second pass checks appeal completeness and filing deadline before it goes out.
05
Deliver
Plain-English status: what's pending, what's won, what's declined and why.
06
Bill
Contingency, 20–30% of recovered amount, invoiced only after the payer pays.
