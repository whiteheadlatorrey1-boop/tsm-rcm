Workforce Readiness & Talent Validation

"This person completed a course" becomes "This person demonstrated proficiency."

For staffing firms, TSM isn't a learning platform — it's a production-simulation environment that turns résumés into evidence.

Candidate Journey — Healthcare Staffing Example
  1. Candidate applies for a Medical Biller position.
  2. Recruiter invites them into the TSM Healthcare Revenue Cycle War Room.
  3. Candidate processes simulated claims, resolves denials, answers realistic scenarios.
  4. TSM generates a competency report: accuracy, productivity, decision quality, compliance awareness, AI-workflow proficiency, overall readiness.
  5. Recruiter shares the report with employers as part of the candidate's profile.
