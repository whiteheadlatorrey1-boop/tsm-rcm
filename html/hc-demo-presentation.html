03 · Exception Management

What's live today — and what's still roadmap

TSMExceptions Engine · Sector-Scoped

The Executive Portal mounts a real exception queue widget scoped to sector: 'healthcare', backed by the shared TSMExceptions engine — the same generic P1/P2/P3 priority model (derived from severity + confidence) also used by the Construction vertical's executive portal, not a one-off.

P1
High severity, ≥90% confidence
P2
High severity, or medium severity at ≥85% confidence
P3
Everything else — open findings below the P1/P2 threshold

Honest status: the queue is mounted and the engine is functional, but no healthcare workflow currently calls TSMExceptions.add() or .fromExplainItems() to feed it — so today the panel renders empty until a producer is wired in. This is different from a fully wired source: it's real infrastructure, not yet populated for this vertical.

Also on the roadmap, not live: cross-sector anomaly propagation. The Executive Portal's own status map shows only Healthcare as ACTIVE; FinOps, Insurance, Construction, Legal, Real Estate, and BPO are listed STANDBY, and "Cross-sector anomaly propagation" is explicitly flagged not-ready in the portal's own autonomous-queue readiness list.
