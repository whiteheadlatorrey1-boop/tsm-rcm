Why This Exists

Most ticketing tools don't know
what they're looking at.

A ticket says "user can't log in." A traditional tool sees text. It has no model of that user's device compliance state, their group membership, the network segment they sit on, or whether the host they're hitting is already flagged. Every diagnosis starts from zero.

Static Knowledge Base

Disconnected Systems
AD, Intune, SCCM, network, and VMware live in separate consoles. The technician manually stitches the picture together.
No Feedback Loop
Resolved tickets vanish — nothing informs which technicians, categories, or root causes are trending.
