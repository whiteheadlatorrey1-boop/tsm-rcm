Command Center — How To Guide

Service Desk Wall & Live Mission Queue

Every open ticket appears as a card on the Service Desk Wall, and the same tickets are sorted by SLA urgency in the Live Mission Queue on the right. Click any ticket card to run it through AI diagnosis and advance it one stage (New → Working → Resolved).

AI Chaos Engine

Click any category button under "AI Chaos Engine — Inject Incident" to generate a new simulated ticket in that category. It lands immediately on the Service Desk Wall and Mission Queue. For categories with a matching digital twin (Dell Laptop, Desktop, Printer, Network, Active Directory, Microsoft 365, VMware), a real fault is also applied to that twin, so SLA Summary, AI Risk Scoring, Technician Performance, and Historical Analytics update too. VPN and SCADA tickets are ticket-only for now — no twin models those systems yet.

Digital Twin Panels (VMware, Network, Active Directory, Device)

Each panel shows the live simulated state of that system — hosts, links, users, endpoints — refreshed automatically every 5 seconds. To inject a fault:

  1. Pick the target entity from the first dropdown (e.g. a specific host, link, or user).
  2. Pick the fault type from the second dropdown (e.g. Host Down, Account Lockout).
  3. Click "Inject Fault." The panel updates immediately, and a matching ticket is created on the Service Desk Wall.

Select "Clear / Reset" and click Inject Fault to return that entity to healthy status.

Scenario Library

Browse every documented fault-response playbook across all six twins. Use the category filters to narrow the list, and click any card to expand it and view the exact step-by-step technician response. Triggering a matching fault above surfaces this same playbook automatically on the resulting ticket.

AI Widget

Type a plain-language description of what you're seeing into the AI Widget box and click "Get Guidance." Groq reasons over the real Scenario Library and returns the single best-matching playbook, its confidence, a one-line explanation of the match, and the full step list — no keyword matching involved.

SLA Summary

Shows how many currently-tracked items are on track, at risk, or breached against their SLA targets, plus the live list of tracked items with time-remaining status. Updates automatically as faults are injected and resolved.

AI Risk Scoring

Every SLA-tracked item is also scored for risk (0–100) and bucketed into Normal / Elevated / Critical. The list is sorted highest-risk first, so the item most likely to need attention next is always at the top.

Technician Performance

A live roster of technicians with assignment counts, resolved counts, currently-open items, and average resolution time in minutes. Assignments are created automatically as the Chaos Engine generates incidents.

Historical Analytics

Snapshots of the whole environment's health are taken automatically over time. The stat row shows the most recent snapshot; the bar chart shows SLA-tracked item totals across recent snapshots — bars turn amber or red when that snapshot had at-risk or breached items.

Notes

This is a simulated environment — no real infrastructure is affected. All data resets when the server restarts. A panel showing an "OFFLINE" badge means its backend isn't reachable; it will keep retrying automatically every 5 seconds.
