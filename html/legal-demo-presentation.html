02 · Relay Chain

How a matter becomes an executive-ready brief

War Room → Strategist → Executive Portal
Real handoff, not a mockup — writes TSM_LEGAL_WAR_RELAY, then TSM_LEGAL_STRATEGIST_RELAY / TSM_STRATEGIST_RELAY
Live Demo Path
STEP 1
War Room triages the matter
The six-engine pipeline runs against the matter and writes TSM_LEGAL_WAR_RELAY, also mirrored to window.TSM.relay.write('LEGAL', ...) on the shared relay bus.
Legal War Room →
STEP 2
Strategist synthesizes & relays
The Main Strategist pulls the War Room brief together and writes both TSM_STRATEGIST_RELAY and the Sentinel-convention TSM_LEGAL_STRATEGIST_RELAY key.
Legal Main Strategist →
STEP 3
Executive Portal surfaces it
Reads across three relay keys in priority order (Sentinel key first, then the shared strategist and war-room keys) so it resolves regardless of which stage last wrote.

03 · Exception Management

What's live today — and what's still roadmap

TSMExceptions Engine · Not Yet Wired for Legal

Unlike Healthcare and Construction, the Legal Executive Portal does not currently mount a TSMExceptionWidget instance. The shared TSMExceptions engine and its generic P1/P2/P3 priority model exist and are already proven in other verticals, but no sector: 'legal' queue is mounted yet.

P1
High severity, ≥90% confidence
P2
High severity, or medium severity at ≥85% confidence
P3
Everything else — open findings below the P1/P2 threshold

Honest status: mounting the widget on the Legal Executive Portal (same pattern already live for Healthcare and Construction) is a scoped, known next step — not a design unknown. It is not represented anywhere on this page as done.
