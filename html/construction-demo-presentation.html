04 · Exception Management

What's live today — and what's still roadmap

TSMExceptions Engine · Sector-Scoped

The Executive Portal mounts a real TSMExceptionWidget scoped to sector: 'construction', backed by the shared TSMExceptions engine — the same generic P1/P2/P3 priority model (derived from severity + confidence) also used by the Healthcare vertical's executive portal, not a one-off.

P1
High severity, ≥90% confidence
P2
High severity, or medium severity at ≥85% confidence
P3
Everything else — open findings below the P1/P2 threshold

Honest status: the queue is mounted and the engine is functional, but no Construction workflow currently calls TSMExceptions.add() or .fromExplainItems() to feed it — so today the panel renders empty until a producer is wired in, the same "plumbed, not fed" state as Healthcare's queue.
