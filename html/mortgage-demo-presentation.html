03 · Exception Management

What's live today — and what's still roadmap

TSMExceptions Engine · Not Yet Wired for Mortgage

The Mortgage Executive Portal does not currently mount a TSMExceptionWidget instance. What it does have, and what most of the batch-1 demo verticals don't, is a real merge-write behind its two action buttons — the same class of fix already shipped for Logistics and Supplier/Vendor's exec portals.

P1
High severity, ≥90% confidence
P2
High severity, or medium severity at ≥85% confidence
P3
Everything else — open findings below the P1/P2 threshold

Honest status: the shared exception queue isn't mounted here yet — that's a known, scoped next step, not a design unknown. The ACKNOWLEDGE/ESCALATE actions are a separate piece of real infrastructure that is already live.
