03 · Exception Management

What's live today — and what's still roadmap

TSMExceptions Engine · Not Yet Wired for Real Estate

The RE Executive Portal does not currently mount a TSMExceptionWidget instance. The shared TSMExceptions engine and its generic P1/P2/P3 priority model exist and are already proven in other verticals, but no sector: 'realestate' queue is mounted yet.

P1
High severity, ≥90% confidence
P2
High severity, or medium severity at ≥85% confidence
P3
Everything else — open findings below the P1/P2 threshold

Honest status: mounting the widget on the RE Executive Portal is a scoped, known next step — not represented anywhere on this page as done.
