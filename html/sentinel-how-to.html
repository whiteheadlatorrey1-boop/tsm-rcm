How To Guide

Using TSM Sentinel Strategist Center

Sentinel is the one board-level view of every vertical your business subscribes to — not another dashboard to drill into, but a standing, ranked view of where risk is concentrated and which vertical is putting pressure on which other one, before you click into anything. This guide walks through every feature, in the order you'll actually use them.

⚠
Sentinel is a 4th-vertical add-on. It only becomes available once your business subscribes to 3 of the 9 core verticals (Schools, Healthcare, FinOps, Insurance, Construction, Legal, Real Estate, BPO, Mortgage). If you're seeing this guide, that gate has already been cleared — the rest of this guide covers what to do once you're in.

ⓘWhat Sentinel Is (and Isn't)

Every vertical you're subscribed to already has its own War Room → Strategist → Executive Portal chain. Sentinel sits above all of them and answers a different question: not "what's wrong in Legal," but "across everything I'm subscribed to, where is risk concentrated, and what's the single highest-leverage thing to fix right now?"

  • It's a rollup, not a replacement. Sentinel reads what each vertical's own Strategist reports — it doesn't re-investigate or re-score the underlying anomalies itself.
  • It's scoped to what you subscribe to. A vertical you haven't subscribed to never contributes a number to anything on this board — see Configure Verticals.
  • It's explainable by design. Every score on this board can be clicked open to see exactly how it was calculated. If a panel says a number is a deterministic formula, that's literal — nothing on this board is a trained model making a judgment call.

1Configure Verticals

This is the first thing to set up, because everything else on the board reads through it. Click ⚙ Configure Verticals in the top right at any time.

  1. Click Configure Verticals in the top bar.
  2. Check every vertical this business actually subscribes to. Unchecking a vertical doesn't delete anything — it just removes it from Sentinel's scoring and view.
  3. Changes apply immediately — the board re-renders as soon as you check or uncheck a box.

If a vertical isn't subscribed, it still appears on the Standings board — dimmed, with a 🔒 Locked badge and no numbers, instead of just disappearing. Clicking a locked row opens this same Configure Verticals panel, so upgrading is always one click away from wherever you notice the gap.

Why this matters
Business Posture Score, Enterprise Exposure, the Standings board, and the Causality Board all read through the same subscription scope. A vertical you haven't subscribed to can never drag your score down, and it can never take credit for improving it either.

2The KPI Row

Four numbers at the top of the board, refreshed every time Sentinel re-renders:

KPIWhat it means
Business Posture Score0–100 composite score, weighted by how much active dollar exposure each subscribed vertical is carrying. Click it — see Business Posture Score below.
Enterprise ExposureSum of every active anomaly's dollar exposure, across your subscribed verticals only.
Verticals At RiskCount of subscribed verticals currently scoring above a risk threshold of 40.
Top Cross-Vertical ThreatThe single anomaly currently placing the most combined risk pressure on your other subscribed verticals — not just the biggest number in isolation.

Under the Business Posture Score you'll sometimes see a small trend line, e.g. ▲ +6 vs 3h ago. Sentinel quietly records a snapshot of your score roughly once an hour in this browser and compares your current score against the oldest one it has from the last 7 days — so over a normal work week you'll start seeing whether posture is actually improving, not just where it stands right now.

Reading the trend
No trend line shown just means there isn't enough history yet in this browser — it appears automatically once at least two snapshots exist and the score has actually moved.

3Business Posture Score, Explained

Click anywhere on the Business Posture Score card to open its explainability panel. This is the "show your work" view — nothing about this score is a black box.

What's inside the panel
  • Formula — Business Posture Score = 100 minus the sum of (each vertical's risk score × its share of your total active exposure). A vertical carrying more of your enterprise's active dollar exposure drags the score down more per point of its own risk.
  • Per-vertical contribution — every subscribed vertical listed, largest drag on the score first, with its risk score and its exposure share shown side by side.
  • Result — the current score, color-coded (green ≥70, amber ≥45, red below).
  • What this is not — an explicit statement that this is a deterministic weighted formula over each vertical's reported severity and exposure figures, not a trained model, and that Sentinel does not re-score or verify the underlying numbers itself.

This is also the honest boundary of the score: if a vertical's own Strategist is reporting wrong numbers, Sentinel will faithfully roll up that wrong number. Sentinel scores what's reported, it doesn't audit it.

4Cross-Vertical Causality Board

This is the direct answer to "what should be done here to improve those?" — not just a risk score, but the actual chain: which anomaly, in which vertical, is putting pressure on which other vertical, and why.

Reading a row

Construction → Legal   Expired permit on Site 4 HIGH

Continued work on an expired permit is a direct code-compliance violation, exposing Legal to stop-work orders and potential litigation. Verified

-15pt

  • Path — which vertical's anomaly is creating pressure, and which vertical is absorbing it.
  • Why — the specific mechanism, in plain language.
  • Verified — that vertical's own Strategist explicitly reported this exact causal reason.
  • Modeled — no explicit reason was reported for this ripple; Sentinel is showing a default weighting rather than inventing a justification. Treat Modeled rows as directional, not as something to quote verbatim in a decision memo.
  • Points — how much relief clearing that one anomaly would give back to Business Posture Score.

Only the top 8 causal chains are shown, ranked by point impact, and only between verticals you're actually subscribed to — a ripple into a vertical you don't have isn't something you can act on.

5Strategist Standings

One row per subscribed vertical, ranked by whatever order your Strategists are reporting in. Each row shows:

ColumnWhat it tells you
Status pillLIVE real data from that vertical's Strategist relay  ·  SAMPLE no relay received yet, showing placeholder data  ·  STALE live data, but the relay hasn't updated in over 24 hours
Top anomalyThat vertical's highest-severity active issue, at a glance.
Risk bar0–100 risk score for that vertical alone.
ExposureTotal dollar exposure of that vertical's active anomalies.
Exec Portal linkOpens that vertical's own Executive Portal, read-only, in a new tab — the full BNCA-level report behind the summary row. Only appears once the vertical is reporting LIVE.

Hover the status pill to see exactly when that vertical's relay last updated — this is the freshness/provenance stamp, and it's the fastest way to spot a vertical that's gone quiet before it shows up as a bigger problem elsewhere.

Click anywhere on a row to expand it and see every anomaly that vertical's Strategist has reported, cleared or not, with severity, confidence, and exposure. Click any individual anomaly to open its full BNCA Impact Analysis.

6BNCA Impact Analysis

Click any anomaly in an expanded row to open this panel — it's where Sentinel earns its name. This is a projection of how clearing this one issue ripples across the rest of your subscribed verticals.

  1. Severity / Exposure / Confidence — the raw facts as reported.
  2. Root Cause — one sentence, from the source Strategist.
  3. Collective Strategist Recommendation — the recommended action, plus what it clears directly and what it relieves elsewhere, in one readable sentence.
  4. Projected Cross-Vertical Relief If Cleared — a bar per affected vertical showing how many points of relief they'd get, each tagged Verified or Modeled the same way the Causality Board is.
  5. Business Posture Impact — current score vs. what it would become if this specific anomaly were cleared.
  6. Simulate Clearing — click to mark the anomaly cleared for this browser session only. The board updates immediately, including Business Posture Score, so you can see the effect before committing to anything in the real vertical system.
Simulation, not action
"Simulate Clearing" never changes anything in the source vertical's War Room or Strategist — it's a local what-if. Use Undo Simulation on a cleared anomaly, or the Reset Simulation button on the main banner, to clear all simulated states at once.

7Live vs. Sample Data

Until a vertical's Strategist writes real data to Sentinel, that vertical shows realistic sample data grounded in that vertical's actual terminology, so the board is legible during onboarding rather than empty. The banner at the top of the board always tells you which mode you're in.

A vertical switches to LIVE automatically the moment its Strategist writes to Sentinel's relay — no manual step needed on your end. If a vertical you've subscribed to still says SAMPLE after go-live, that's worth flagging to your platform admin; it usually means that vertical's Strategist page hasn't been wired to Sentinel's relay key yet.

8Uploading a Relay Export

For one-off testing, demos, or a vertical that isn't yet wired for automatic live relay, you can manually load a relay export file:

  1. Click Upload Relay Export in the banner.
  2. Choose a .json file matching the relay payload shape (your platform admin can provide this, or export one from a vertical's own Strategist page if it supports export).
  3. If the file doesn't already specify which vertical it's for, you'll be prompted for the vertical id.
  4. You'll see a confirmation banner with a direct link into that vertical's Executive Portal for the full detail.

This is a manual override for that vertical's data in this browser only — it doesn't publish anything back to the source vertical, and it's overwritten the next time that vertical's real relay updates.

10Export Snapshot

Click Export Snapshot in the banner to download a standalone, self-contained HTML file capturing the board exactly as it looks at that moment: Business Posture Score, Enterprise Exposure, Verticals At Risk, the full Standings table, and the Causality Board — including the Verified/Modeled tags.

  • Print-friendly — light theme by design, since the dark terminal UI doesn't print well; drop it straight into a board packet or print it.
  • Self-contained — no dependency on Sentinel being open or on this session's data; it's a fixed record of that moment.
  • Carries the same disclaimer language as the BPS panel, so anyone reading the exported file without context still sees what the score is and isn't.

Use this any time you need to hand the board's current state to someone without Sentinel access, or before a decision that references these numbers — the file itself is timestamped proof of what the board said at that point.

11Alerting

Sentinel can proactively flag a posture drop instead of waiting for someone to open the tab. Configure it from the Configure Verticals panel:

  1. Alert threshold — set the Business Posture Score floor. Leave it blank to turn alerting off.
  2. Webhook URL (optional) — if set, Sentinel best-effort sends a small JSON payload ({ bps, threshold, at }) to this URL the moment the score first crosses below your floor.

When the threshold is crossed, you'll see a red banner at the top of the board, and it won't fire again for the same dip — it resets once the score recovers back above the floor, so you get one alert per drop, not one per page refresh.

If a webhook fails
A failed webhook delivery never blocks the board or the in-page banner — the banner and the record in the Audit Log are always the reliable source, treat the webhook as a convenience notification on top of that.

12Audit Log

Click 📜 Audit Log in the top bar to see a running, append-only record of what's changed on this board: who added or removed a subscribed vertical, when an alert threshold was crossed or recovered, when alerting was configured, and every time a snapshot was exported.

  • Every entry is timestamped and cannot be edited or deleted once written — only new entries get added.
  • Use Export Log inside the panel to download the full log as a .json file — useful alongside an exported snapshot as supporting evidence for a specific decision.
Know its limits
This log lives in this browser's local storage — it's per-device, not a shared or centralized record. It proves what Sentinel displayed and changed on this machine; it is not a tamper-proof, server-side audit trail. If you need a record that survives a cleared browser or that's independently verifiable across devices, that's a separate, server-side capability to request from your platform admin — treat this log as a first line of evidence, not the final word.

?FAQ & Troubleshooting

A vertical I subscribe to isn't showing up at all.
Check Configure Verticals first — it may simply not be checked. If it's checked and still missing, that's a platform issue to raise with your admin rather than something fixable from this board.
Why does my Business Posture Score change when I didn't clear anything?
BPS recalculates live from whatever each vertical's Strategist is currently reporting. If a vertical's underlying numbers changed — a new anomaly, a resolved one, an updated exposure figure — BPS reflects that automatically.
What's the difference between clearing an anomaly here and clearing it in the vertical's own War Room?
Clearing here (Simulate Clearing) is a local preview only — it never touches the source vertical. To actually resolve an anomaly, use that vertical's own Executive Portal or War Room.
Is my data shared with other businesses using Sentinel?
No — Sentinel only reads relay data for the verticals your business is subscribed to. Nothing here is pooled or compared across other businesses on the platform.
Can I get an alert by email instead of the in-page banner?
Not directly — the built-in alert is a webhook POST (see Alerting). If you need email delivery, point the webhook at an automation (e.g. an email-on-webhook integration) rather than expecting Sentinel to send email itself.
I can't find the "Ask Sentinel" answers I expected.
See Ask Sentinel above — that feature isn't wired up yet in this build. Use the BPS Explainability panel and Causality Board for the same underlying answers today.
