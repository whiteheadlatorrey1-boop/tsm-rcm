AI Analyst Moment

“The AI Analyst is not answering a generic question. It is determining risk, owner lane, SLA pressure, missing evidence, and the next operational action.”

Expected BNCA

TOP ISSUE
Operational blocker detected.

RISK LEVEL
Medium to High depending on SLA age, compliance exposure, and evidence gaps.

BEST NEXT ACTIONS
1. Assign accountable owner.
2. Validate supporting documents.
3. Clear blocker older than SLA.
4. Escalate unresolved issue to Strategist.

CONFIDENCE
Demo target: 88–94%

Operating Lane Narrative

“This is where invisible work becomes managed work. Every queue now has a lane, owner, priority, evidence trail, and escalation path.”

Executive Rollup

  • Queue visibility
  • SLA pressure
  • Owner accountability
  • Risk concentration
  • Revenue, compliance, or leakage impact

Why executives care

Executives and office managers see denial risk, prior-auth aging, intake pressure, missing documentation, AR exposure, and compliance escalation before revenue is lost.

30-Day Pilot Ask

“For the pilot, we start with one workflow, one queue, and one measurable outcome. We do not replace your systems. We add the operating intelligence layer above them.”

POC Metrics

  • Manual review time reduction
  • Faster escalation
  • SLA visibility
  • Reduced leakage or compliance exposure
  • Weekly executive rollup
