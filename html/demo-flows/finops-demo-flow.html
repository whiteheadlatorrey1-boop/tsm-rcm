AI Analyst Moment

“The AI Analyst is determining risk, owner lane, SLA pressure, evidence gaps, and the best next action.”

Expected BNCA

TOP ISSUE
Operational blocker detected.

RISK LEVEL
Medium to High depending on SLA age and evidence gaps.

BEST NEXT ACTIONS
1. Assign accountable owner.
2. Validate document evidence.
3. Clear blocker older than SLA.
4. Escalate unresolved issue to Strategist.

CONFIDENCE
Demo target: 88–94%

Operating Lane Narrative

“This is where the work stops being invisible. Each queue now has an owner, status, risk score, and next action.”

Executive Rollup

  • Queue visibility
  • SLA pressure
  • Owner accountability
  • Risk concentration
  • Recovery or leakage opportunity

Why executives care

Executives see invoice variance, close blockers, reconciliation exposure, and recovery opportunities before month-end.

30-Day Pilot Ask

“For the pilot, we start with one workflow, one queue, and one measurable outcome. We do not replace your systems. We add the decision layer above them.”

POC Metrics

  • Manual review time reduction
  • Faster escalation
  • SLA visibility
  • Recovered leakage / avoided risk
  • Weekly executive rollup
