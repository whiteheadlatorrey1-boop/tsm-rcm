What's in HotelOps, and how to use it

This is the working guide for the HotelOps War Room — every tab, what it's tracking, and where the numbers come from. If you'd rather ask than read, the 🤖 assistant in the bottom-right of the War Room can answer any of this directly, plus give you a live, ranked briefing of what needs attention right now.

Reservations Front Desk VIP Arrivals Housekeeping Maintenance IoT / Smart Systems Staff Operations Incident Center OTA Intelligence Revenue Management Compliance Airbnb / STR Data & Relay Flow AI Assistant

Reservations sidebar → Reservations

Flags bookings that need action before arrival: failed payments, unconfirmed bookings close to check-in, and waitlist risk.

  • What to do: re-run failed cards, chase unconfirmed guests, manage the waitlist before rooms lock.

Front Desk sidebar → Front Desk

The live front-desk queue — check-ins, check-outs, and requests — with anything that's breached its target handling time called out.

VIP Arrivals sidebar → VIP Arrivals

Readiness gaps for VIP guests: amenities, room prep, or arrival notes that haven't been confirmed complete yet.

Housekeeping sidebar → Housekeeping

Room-turn tasks and any that have breached their target turnaround time — the list housekeeping supervisors should be working from.

Maintenance sidebar → Maintenance

Open maintenance tickets, with SLA-breached and urgent-severity tickets surfaced first. This feeds directly into the KPI bar ("OPEN MAINT TICKETS") and the assistant's briefing.

IoT / Smart Systems sidebar → IoT / Smart Systems

Live sensor alerts (leak, temperature, access, energy) plus energy usage summary and predictive-risk signals from the building management system.

You can also paste a sensor export (from your BMS/IoT platform) into the sidebar importer on the War Room — Parse Preview, then Import to War Room — instead of entering readings by hand.

Staff Operations sidebar → Staff Operations

Staffing gaps by shift/department — where you're short-handed and by how much.

Incident Center sidebar → Incident Center

Open guest/property incidents, with anything past its response-time SLA flagged for immediate duty-manager escalation.

Escalations created upstream (Strategist or Sentinel) show up here automatically once they touch this property — tagged LIVE MISSION with their current stage. There's no "create mission" button in the War Room itself; this panel is a live read of what's already been escalated elsewhere.

OTA Intelligence sidebar → OTA Intelligence

Commission audit against OTA (Expedia/Booking.com) contracted rates — this period's overcharge total and the annualized exposure estimate.

Revenue Management sidebar → Revenue Management

RevPAR, ADR, occupancy, and GOP margin trends — the same figures shown in the top KPI bar, broken out in more depth here.

Compliance sidebar → Compliance

Items due within the compliance window — I-9 re-verification, licensing, fire-safety and health-permit renewals — so nothing lapses unnoticed.

Any compliance-classified mission already open elsewhere in the system appears here too, tagged LIVE, so this list reflects both locally-tracked items and anything already being worked upstream.

Airbnb / STR sidebar → Airbnb / STR

Risk flags across your short-term-rental listings: overdue turnovers ahead of the next check-in, unsynced OTA calendars (double-booking risk), lockbox/access codes that haven't been rotated since the last guest, and host response times over the 60-minute SLA. Also surfaces any IoT sensor alert (leak, offline device, temperature drift) tied to a specific listing.

  • What to do: clear overdue turnovers before the next arrival, re-sync any listing calendar flagged out of date, rotate lockbox codes between guests, and follow up on any host response over SLA.

Data & Relay Flow sidebar → DATA panel

Three controls in the sidebar govern where your data comes from and where it goes:

  • LOAD SAMPLE DATA — populates the War Room with representative sample records so you can explore before real data is wired in.
  • RESET SAVED DATA — clears anything saved locally and reloads sample data from scratch.
  • RELAY TO STRATEGIST → — pushes the current War Room snapshot (KPIs, anomalies, AI notes) to the HotelOps Strategist, which is what the Executive Portal ultimately rolls up. The payload is sealed with a SHA-256 integrity check before it's sent, so Strategist, Executive Portal, and Sentinel Center can all detect if it was altered in transit.
  • Sentinel Center — the same relay push carries a ready-made anomalies array (maintenance SLA breaches and similar signals) in the format Sentinel Center expects, so cross-property severity rolls up there without any separate setup.

🤖 Using the AI Assistant

The assistant lives in the bottom-right corner of the War Room. Tap it and it opens straight to a ranked briefing of what actually needs attention right now — pulled from the same live numbers as this page, not a canned greeting.

From there you can also just ask it things in plain language — "what needs attention today," "any SLA breaches," "how do I use the IoT tab," or "what does OTA Intelligence show." It's grounded in both the live workspace data and everything on this guide, so it can point you to the right tab as well as tell you what's currently wrong.
