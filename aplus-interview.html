A+ Interview Mode
"Walk me through how you'd handle this" interview walkthroughs. Every response you'd give is scored on two independent axes — technical soundness AND how you'd actually communicate it, out loud, to a user or interviewer — because a candidate can be technically right and communicate it badly, or vice versa. Both scores feed the same shared mastery data behind every other A+ tool, tracked separately so a weak communicator with strong technical instincts (or the reverse) shows up clearly.
