TSM PROSPECT PROOF PACK
========================

Five synthetic, prospect-ready document bundles are included as single PDF packets.

IMPORTANT:
All information is synthetic demonstration data. It is not a real customer, patient,
borrower, property, claim, account, or business record.

Recommended demonstration sequence:
1. Give prospect the relevant packet before the demo.
2. Ask: "If this landed in your team's inbox today, what would they have to do?"
3. Ingest the packet into the appropriate TSM War Room.
4. Show extraction/classification and priority.
5. Show the structured case and evidence.
6. Show recommendation / decision support.
7. Show delivery package.
8. Show HITL approval.
9. Show mission tracking.
10. Show Executive Portal / Sentinel rollup.

Suggested mapping:
- Healthcare -> Healthcare Denial War Room
- Mortgage -> Mortgage War Room
- Construction -> Construction War Room
- BPO -> BPO Recovery Desk
- Hotel/PM -> HotelOps / PM Copilot

The packets intentionally contain:
- multiple related documents/information sources
- inconsistencies or unresolved conditions
- deadlines
- financial/operational exposure
- evidence that can be correlated
- human decision points
- a plausible next action

The expected outputs printed inside each packet are demonstration targets, not claims
about what TSM will produce without running the actual workflow.
